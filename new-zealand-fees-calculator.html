How Etsy fees work in New Zealand

Every sale on Etsy includes a listing fee of US$0.20 (shown in New Zealand dollars), a 6.5% transaction fee on the item price plus shipping, and payment processing of 3% + NZ$0.30 on domestic orders and 4% + NZ$0.30 on international orders. Etsy does not currently list a regulatory operating fee for New Zealand sellers, so it is set to 0%. Tick the GST box if Etsy charges 15% GST on the fees in your payment account. Offsite Ads add another 12-15% only when a sale comes through those ads.

Example: you sell a NZ$40 item and charge NZ$8 shipping, so the order total is NZ$48.00. Etsy fees come to about NZ$5.21 (10.9% of the order), leaving a net payout of NZ$42.79. After your item cost (NZ$12.00) and postage (NZ$8.00), your profit is about NZ$22.79, a margin of 47.5%. Change the numbers above to see your own result.

Tip: Transaction and processing fees apply to shipping too, so charging NZ$10 shipping on a NZ$40 item means Etsy also takes a cut of that NZ$10.

FAQ

How much does Etsy take from a NZ$40 sale in New Zealand?

On a NZ$40 item with NZ$8 shipping, Etsy's fees come to about 11% of the order at the default settings, and more if the sale comes from Offsite Ads. Use the calculator above for your exact numbers.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The transaction fee and payment processing fee are calculated on the full order total, including shipping.

Can I avoid Offsite Ads fees?

Shops under $10,000 in annual sales can opt out. Larger shops cannot, and pay a reduced 12% rate.
