How to price Etsy items for profit

A simple formula that includes fees, costs and your target margin

Many sellers price by guessing or copying competitors, then discover Etsy fees ate their margin. A better way is to work backwards from the profit you want.

Step 1: add up your costs

Include materials, your time if you want to pay yourself, packaging, postage you pay, and any fixed Etsy fees (listing fee and the fixed part of payment processing).

Step 2: add the percentage fees

For a UK shop, the main percentage fees are 6.5% transaction, 4% processing and about 0.48% regulatory, which is roughly 11%. With 20% VAT on Etsy's fees, that becomes about 13.2% of the order.

Step 3: use this formula

Price = (costs + fixed fees) ÷ (1 − fee rate − profit margin)

Example: materials £8, postage £3 and packaging £1 give costs of £12. Fixed fees are about £0.43 including VAT (£0.16 listing + £0.20 processing, times 1.2). You want a 30% margin and the fee rate is 13.2%.

Price = £12.43 ÷ (1 − 0.132 − 0.30) = £12.43 ÷ 0.568 = about £21.88. Check: fees are about £3.32, costs £12, so profit is about £6.56, which is 30%.

Tips

  • Free shipping is not free: build postage into the price, and remember fees apply to the shipping you charge.
  • Cheap items suffer most from fixed fees, so a £4 item loses a bigger share than a £40 one.
  • Leave room for sales, discounts and Offsite Ads.
