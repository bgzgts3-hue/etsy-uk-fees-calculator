How Etsy fees work in Canada

Every sale on Etsy includes a listing fee of US$0.20 (shown in your payment currency), a 6.5% transaction fee on the item price plus shipping, and payment processing. For sellers with a Canadian bank account, processing is 3% + C$0.25 on orders from Canada and the US, and 4% + C$0.25 on orders from other countries. Canadian sellers also pay a regulatory operating fee on the order total. Offsite Ads add another 12-15% only when a sale comes through those ads.

Tip: Transaction and processing fees apply to shipping too, so charging C$10 shipping on a C$30 item means Etsy also takes a cut of that C$10.

FAQ

How much does Etsy take from a C$30 sale in Canada?

Roughly 12-14% of the order once listing, transaction, processing and regulatory fees are included, more if the sale comes from Offsite Ads. Use the calculator above for your exact numbers.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The transaction fee, payment processing fee and regulatory fee are all calculated on the full order total, including shipping.

Can I avoid Offsite Ads fees?

Shops under $10,000 in annual sales can opt out. Larger shops cannot, and pay a reduced 12% rate.
