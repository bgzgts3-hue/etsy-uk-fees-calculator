How much does Etsy take per sale?

Typical Etsy fees in the UK for a standard sale

On a typical UK sale, Etsy takes roughly 12-15% of the order total (item price plus shipping), and more if the sale comes from Offsite Ads. It is not one fee but several that stack together.

The fees on every sale

FeeRate
Listing feeUS$0.20 (about £0.16) per listing
Transaction fee6.5% of item + shipping
Payment processing (UK)4% + £0.20 per order
Regulatory operating feeabout 0.48% of order (UK, from 22 June 2026)
VAT on fees20%, unless you give Etsy a UK VAT number

Rates checked in October 2026. Etsy changes fees from time to time, so confirm on Etsy's official fees page.

Worked example: £25 item + £3.50 shipping

The order total is £28.50. The listing fee is £0.16, the transaction fee £1.85, processing £1.34 (4% = £1.14 + £0.20) and the regulatory fee about £0.14. That is £3.49 before VAT. With 20% VAT on top (£0.70), Etsy takes about £4.19, or 14.7% of the order, leaving a payout of roughly £24.31.

What can push fees higher

  • Offsite Ads: an extra 15% (12% for shops over US$10,000 a year) only when a sale comes through those ads. See are Offsite Ads worth it?
  • Currency conversion: 2.5% if your listing currency differs from your payment account currency.
  • Shipping: the 6.5% and 4% apply to shipping you charge too.
