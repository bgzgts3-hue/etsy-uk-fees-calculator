How the Etsy Offsite Ads fee works

Offsite Ads are Etsy's adverts on Google, Facebook, Instagram and other sites. You only pay when a buyer clicks one of these ads and buys within 30 days. The fee is 15% of the order total (item + shipping) for shops under $10,000 in annual sales, or 12% for larger shops, which cannot opt out.

This fee comes on top of the normal listing, transaction (6.5%) and payment processing fees, which is why a sale with ads can leave you with almost no profit on low-margin items.

FAQ

Can I turn off Offsite Ads?

Yes, if your shop earned under $10,000 in the last 365 days. Once you pass that, they are compulsory at the lower 12% rate.

Is the Offsite Ads fee charged on shipping?

Yes, it is calculated on the full order total including shipping.

Is there a maximum Offsite Ads fee?

Yes, Etsy caps the fee per item at around $100.
