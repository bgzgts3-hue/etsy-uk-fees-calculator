How Etsy fees work in Australia

Every sale on Etsy includes a listing fee of US$0.20 (shown in your payment currency), a 6.5% transaction fee on the item price plus shipping, and payment processing. For sellers with an Australian bank account, processing is 3% + A$0.25 on domestic orders and 4% + A$0.25 on international orders. Etsy also adds 10% GST to its fees, which GST-registered sellers can often claim back. Offsite Ads add another 12-15% only when a sale comes through those ads.

Tip: Transaction and processing fees apply to shipping too, so charging A$10 shipping on a A$30 item means Etsy also takes a cut of that A$10.

FAQ

How much does Etsy take from an A$30 sale in Australia?

Roughly 13-15% of the order once listing, transaction, processing and GST on fees are included, more if the sale comes from Offsite Ads. Use the calculator above for your exact numbers.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The transaction fee and payment processing fee are calculated on the full order total, including shipping.

Can I avoid Offsite Ads fees?

Shops under US$10,000 in annual sales can opt out. Larger shops cannot, and pay a reduced 12% rate.
