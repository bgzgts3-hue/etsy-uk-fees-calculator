Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This site offers free calculators that help Etsy sellers estimate fees and profit. This page explains what information is and is not collected when you use it.

What you type into the calculators

The numbers you enter (prices, costs, fees) are processed in your browser only. They are not sent to our servers, stored, or shared.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, address or payment details. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message, and we use them only to reply to you.

Hosting and logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you load a page. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Cookies and advertising

We may show ads on this site now or in the future. Advertising partners, such as Google AdSense or other ad networks, may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. They may collect information such as your IP address and browsing activity under their own privacy policies. We do not control those cookies.

You can manage or turn off personalised ads through your browser settings or Google's Ads Settings, and you can learn more at aboutads.info. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent before ad cookies are used.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics or Google Search Console to understand how many people visit the site and which pages are useful. These tools show us overall figures and do not identify you personally.

External links

Some pages link to other websites, such as Etsy. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children

This site is meant for adults who sell online. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
