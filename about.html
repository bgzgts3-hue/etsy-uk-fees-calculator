About

Free, simple calculators for Etsy sellers.

Etsy fees are easy to lose track of: listing, transaction, payment processing, regulatory fees, VAT or GST on fees, and Offsite Ads. Together they can take a bigger share of a sale than most sellers expect. These calculators show where the money goes, so you can price your items with your real profit in mind.

  • Etsy digital downloads profit calculator

How the numbers are worked out

Each calculator uses the fee rates Etsy publishes for sellers in that country. Every rate can be edited in the fee settings, so you can correct it if Etsy changes its pricing or your shop is on different terms. Results are estimates for planning, not accounting or tax advice. Always check Etsy's official fees page for current rates.
