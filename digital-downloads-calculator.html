How Etsy fees work on digital downloads

Every sale of a digital file carries a listing fee, a transaction fee on the item price, and a payment processing fee. If a buyer arrived through an Offsite Ad, Etsy also takes an Offsite Ads fee on the order total. Because digital items usually have low prices, the fixed parts of the fees (listing fee and the fixed processing amount) take a bigger share than on physical products.

Frequently asked questions

Do I pay shipping costs on digital downloads?
No. There is nothing to ship, so your costs are Etsy fees plus anything you spend creating or promoting the file.

Why does a cheap printable leave so little profit?
The fixed fees apply to each sale regardless of price, so a £2 item loses a much higher percentage than a £20 item. Bundles and higher prices improve your margin.

What is a good profit margin for digital products?
Many sellers aim for 70% or more, since there are no materials or postage. Use the break-even price above to check your floor.
