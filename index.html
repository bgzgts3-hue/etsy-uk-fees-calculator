How Etsy UK fees work

Every UK sale on Etsy includes a listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee on the item price plus shipping, and payment processing of 4% + £0.20. UK sellers also pay a regulatory operating fee, which Etsy raised to 0.48% in June 2026. If you are not VAT registered, Etsy adds 20% VAT on top of its fees. Offsite Ads add another 12-15% only when a sale comes through those ads.

Tip: Transaction fees apply to shipping too, so charging £5 shipping on a £15 item means Etsy also takes a cut of that £5.

FAQ

How much does Etsy take from a £20 sale in the UK?

Roughly 11-14% of the order once listing, transaction, processing, regulatory and VAT on fees are included. Use the calculator above for your exact numbers.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The transaction fee and payment processing fee are both calculated on item price plus shipping.

Can I avoid Offsite Ads fees?

Shops under $10,000 in annual sales can opt out. Larger shops cannot, and pay a reduced 12% rate.
