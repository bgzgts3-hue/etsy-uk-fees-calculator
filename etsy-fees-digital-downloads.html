Etsy fees for digital downloads

Why low-priced printables lose a bigger share and what to do about it

Digital downloads pay the same Etsy fees as physical products: listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee, payment processing and regulatory fee. There is no shipping to pay, but the fixed fees still apply to every sale.

Example: a £5 printable (UK)

Listing £0.16, transaction £0.33, processing £0.40 (4% + £0.20) and regulatory about £0.02 total £0.91. With 20% VAT on fees, Etsy takes about £1.09, or 21.8%, leaving £3.91.

Example: a £12 bundle

The same items sold as a bundle cost about £2.01 in fees with VAT, which is 16.8%, leaving £9.99. Bigger order values dilute the fixed fees.

How to improve your margin

  • Sell bundles and sets instead of single cheap files.
  • Raise the price of your best sellers a little rather than discounting.
  • Remember the listing fee is charged again at renewal and each time a multi-quantity listing sells.
  • Your costs are mostly time and software, so track them too.
